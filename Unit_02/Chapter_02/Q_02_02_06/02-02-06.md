# Unit 2 Chapter 2 Question 6 - Federal Tax Bracket Finder
Navigating the Canadian tax system can be complex! One of the first steps is understanding which federal tax bracket your annual income falls into. This helps determine the rate at which your income is taxed at the federal level.

You are given a yearly salary and need to determine which of the five federal tax brackets it belongs to based on the 2026 tax year ranges.

Here are the 2026 Canadian Federal Tax Brackets (as per [the CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/tax-rates-brackets/current-year.html)):

| Bracket Number | Taxable Income Range                     |
| :------------- | :--------------------------------------- |
| 1              | up to $58,523                            |
| 2              | $58,523.01 up to $117,045            |
| 3              | over$117,045.01 up to $181,440            |
| 4              | over $181,440.01 up to $258,482            |
| 5              | $258,482.01 or more                           |

Your task is to take a given salary and identify the corresponding bracket number (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).

Let the yearly salary be represented by $S$.

## Input Specification
The input consists of a single line containing a non-negative number, $S$, representing the yearly salary. $S$ will only be a whole number.

## Output Specification
Output a single integer from 1 to 5, representing the federal tax bracket number that the salary $S$ falls into based on the 2024 ranges provided above.

## Sample Input 1

```
50000
```

## Sample Output 1

```
1
```

## Explanation for Sample Output 1

A salary of $\$50,000$ is less than or equal to $\$58,523$, placing it in the first tax bracket.

## Sample Input 2

```
111000
```

## Sample Output 2

```
2
```

## Explanation for Sample Output 2

A salary of $\$111,000$ is greater than or equal to $\$58,523.01$ and less than or equal to $\$117,045$. Therefore, it falls into the second tax bracket.
